# Research context

Literature and project evidence reviewed through 26 November 2026. Published measurements retain their original participant, split and decoder conditions. The local [v1](../reports/results.md) and [v2](../reports/continuation.md) reports describe what this project measured.

T15 comes from the attempted-speech neuroprosthesis described by [Card et al., NEJM 2024](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2314132). The [July 2025 Dryad release](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.dncjsxm85) provides processed intracortical features and a separate pretrained GRU. T12/Brain-to-Text '24, T15/Brain-to-Text '25 and later clinical use have different data and evaluation conditions.

The [official PyTorch recipe](https://github.com/Neuroprosthetics-Lab/nejm-brain-to-text/blob/980a5b7e96392210edf976d76bdb5d15d2e0e62b/model_training/README.md) anchors recovery: day-specific transforms, five 768-unit GRU layers and phoneme CTC. Its approximately 10.1% validation PER and 3.5-hour RTX 4090 training time describe the authors' recipe. They do not set a local acceptance threshold or predict laptop runtime. The supplied trainer uses same-padded smoothing, while the inference helper uses valid smoothing; numerical comparisons must identify which path they follow.

The v1 studies asked whether fixed word candidates benefit from confidence-gated extra scoring, and whether a frozen encoder benefits from head or session-bias calibration. V2 investigated the candidate coverage left by native search, larger neural priors, in-search fusion and alternative encoder objectives. V1 reporting results informed v2, so v2 scores on the existing benchmark are exploratory even when a new artifact freeze precedes scoring.

## Context-dependent phones and alternative encoders

[Li et al., DCoND v1, November 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.10657v1) model a joint diphone distribution and marginalize it to ordinary phone probabilities. On T12 they report 15.34% PER versus 16.62% for monophones. The 5.77% WER belongs to DCoND-LIFT, including additional language-model stages.

The completed local experiment adapts that objective to T15 with a frozen GRU, a full 40-by-40 joint head, separate blank, and transition/self-pair targets. A matched monophone control used the same eligible training data and 1,024 updates. Diphone adaptation worsened exploratory report WER from 19.93% to 22.63%, a change of +2.70 points with a paired session-block interval of +1.90 to +3.40. This measures that particular adaptation; it does not reproduce or refute the full T12 DCoND-LIFT system. [Local encoder results](../reports/continuation.md#alternative-encoder-objectives)

[Feghhi et al., v2, November 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.02800v2) study causal time-masked Transformers and DietCORP, which uses language-derived pseudo-labels, augmented copies of the current trial and an input-module update. Their held-out-day protocol differs from supervised updates within known T15 sessions. The initial source audit did not establish a ready author neural checkpoint.

The local Transformer is a separate, bounded experiment trained from scratch. It reached 40,000 updates after checkpoint resume, then scored 56.10% exploratory report WER with the same compact decoder used for its GRU comparison. Whole-pipeline p95 was about 110 ms for both encoders in the matched addendum. This small scratch-trained encoder had substantially worse accuracy than the mature pretrained GRU; its causal attention does not make the offline interface a streaming system.

Cross-species pretraining is a different scale of work. [Zhang et al., BIT v5, May 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.21740v5) combine neural pretraining with cascaded and end-to-end decoding. Appendix R reports encoder pretraining on an A100 80 GB in under two days, followed by task training. The [inspected MIT code release](https://github.com/yzhang511/bit/tree/f107ae988d782609b78d1d7488490cca9fb7a103) did not establish a ready pretrained-weight download during the audit. That reproduction remains separate from the completed local encoder comparisons.

Clinical studies pose broader transfer questions. [Card et al., Nature Medicine 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13375540/) describe prolonged independent use with a Transformer and continued adaptation; the personal-use neural recordings and transcripts are not shared. [Fogg et al., July 2026 preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13483768/) describe shared training across six participants and rapid held-out-user calibration. Their protocols motivate future work beyond the known-session public copy-task benchmark used here.

## Language models during and after search

Fixed-candidate scoring can only select words already present in the saved list. The revision-pinned [DistilGPT2 card](https://huggingface.co/distilbert/distilgpt2/blob/2290a62682d06624634c1f46a6ad5be0f47f38aa/README.md) identifies Apache-2.0 terms, OpenWebText pretraining and GPT-2 distillation. The local scorer generates nothing. V2 also measured Qwen3-1.7B-Base and Qwen3-4B-Base on identical candidates without local task fine-tuning. Incidental benchmark overlap is unknown for all three. DistilGPT2 led on tuning and full validation; the 4B model had two fewer exploratory report edits. Those small differences do not establish that a larger reranker solves the candidate-coverage gap.

[LightBeam v1, March 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.14002v1) integrates lexicon constraints, an n-gram and periodic neural scoring during search. With its baseline GRU, Table 1 reports 5.77% public and 6.47% private T15 WER, 11.6 GB host RAM and 5.8 GB VRAM; timing used an RTX 5090.

Full integration was deferred during initial recovery. The [pinned BrainAudio recipe](https://github.com/ebrahimfeghhi/brainaudio/blob/90e0ac086d4dee643be154f446a6c374600575d7/README.md) required ImagineVille Huge, a 4.9 GB compressed four-gram, and adapted Llama assets that were not verified. Published host usage left little headroom under the laptop's 12 GB target. No root source redistribution license was found in the inspected tree, and [Llama 3.2](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama/Llama-3.2-1B) has separate model terms.

V2 subsequently ran the pinned search backend on 24 fixed tuning trials, using the local training-only three-gram and general-pretrained Qwen3-1.7B-Base. All three arms saved predictions. N-gram-only, final-only Qwen and periodic-plus-final Qwen made 31, 30 and 23 edits over the same 156 reference words. Repaired compact search also made 23 edits. Periodic fusion's cached-logit p95 was about 1.98 s, versus 0.79 s for final-only scoring. This completed feasibility comparison does not reproduce the paper's asset configuration or establish a broader WER improvement. [LightBeam measurements](../reports/continuation.md#lightbeam-with-in-search-fusion)

Text exposure also matters when comparing the paper. Section 3.4.2 says next-word tuning uses train plus validation sentences, while Appendix F describes training text with validation tuning. Appendix G explicitly uses validation text for generative correction. We therefore did not treat imported adapted weights as independent of local validation.

The [ImagineVille release](https://imagineville.org/software/lm/dec19/) is licensed CC BY 4.0. V2 acquired its standalone Small four-gram for a separate first-pass comparison. Both sustained pilots stopped on native request timeouts after about ten warm minutes, with zero OOM events, and produced no admitted tuning WER. Compressed size, loaded memory and sustained service behavior are different measurements.

[BEST v1, October 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.23175v1) explores hierarchical phone, character and audio-compatible representations. Its 1.62% T15 test result uses a five-gram; the main table assigns that configuration 320 GiB. Runnable released weights were not verified in this audit, and the benchmark has not been reproduced here.

## What the present evidence supports

The native trie repair and wider search improved tuning candidate coverage with the same GRU emissions and training-only three-gram. The tuning-selected repaired beam-250 system with DistilGPT2 reached 14.80% exploratory report WER and 13.84% on full validation. Its matched complete-request p95 was 285 ms on a PCIe A100 allocation. The report retains the harmful diphone and Transformer comparisons, random-word results, paired intervals and the distinction between incremental scoring and complete-pipeline timing. The 10% WER target is still open. [Measured continuation](../reports/continuation.md)

Paired-electrode masking remains deferred for a separate reason: the column map is ambiguous. The [organizer feature description](https://github.com/Neuroprosthetics-Lab/nejm-brain-to-text/blob/980a5b7e96392210edf976d76bdb5d15d2e0e62b/README.md#data) starts one range at 0–64 and ends later ranges at 512 for a 512-column tensor. The inspected HDF5 features had no column identifiers. A paired mask needs an unambiguous electrode-to-column mapping.

New comparisons must preserve their registered data access and exposure history. The original checkpoint's validation selection and the later v1-to-v2 exposure remain part of the interpretation. [DATA_CARD.md](DATA_CARD.md), [MODEL_CARD.md](MODEL_CARD.md) and [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md) define the data, model and source contracts.
